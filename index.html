The problem

Homestays run on scattered data. Iron brings it together.

Inquiries arrive across WhatsApp, calls and OTAs. Owners overbook as a safeguard, then scramble to avoid a clash. Iron gives that process a single, structured home.

Bookings live everywhere

Chats, calls, notebooks and screenshots. There is no single view of who wants which dates.

Overbooking is a gamble

Extra inquiries act as a buffer, but risk an avoidable double-booking when everyone confirms.

The guest is unknown

A stranger books your home with no visibility into how they have treated other hosts.
